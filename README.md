# Protect Probashi

Protect Probashi helps Bangladeshis living and working abroad find useful information in one place. Use it in **Bangla or English** to find help, look up Bangladesh embassy contacts, read expatriate news, compare currencies, and stay connected with home.

## What you can do

### Find emergency help

Look up local emergency numbers and available Bangladesh embassy contacts for your country. Where calling is supported on your device, tap a number to call.

### Find your nearest Bangladesh office

Browse Bangladesh embassies, high commissions, consulates, and permanent missions. Search by country, city, or office name, and view available phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, and staff details.

Open an office to see its details. Opening another office closes the previous one, making the list easier to browse.

Some details are still missing or waiting to be checked. Where a head of mission’s individual contact is shown instead of an office contact, it is labelled. Check the official website before travelling to an office.

### Read about your rights

Find introductory information about workers’ rights and where to seek help. This section is still being developed. For advice about your own situation, contact the relevant authority or a qualified adviser.

### Follow news from home and abroad

Read recent headlines and available summaries from linked publishers, including Prothom Alo. Each story links to the original report so you can read more. Some stories or images may be unavailable when you are offline.

### Convert money to Bangladeshi taka

Choose a currency and enter an amount to see its estimated value in taka. Check the update time shown beside the rates. Your bank or money-transfer service may offer a different amount because of its rates, fees, or incentives.

### Compare local times

Use the world clock to compare the time in Bangladesh with other places. This can help you plan calls with family or keep track of time differences.

### Make your Bangladesh travel map

Mark the districts you have visited, choose a colour theme, and optionally add your name and photo. You can show district names and download your map as an image.

Your map choices are saved in your browser when storage is available, so you can return to them later on the same device and browser.

## Getting started

1. Open Protect Probashi and choose Bangla or English.
2. Select your country and city to find relevant information.
3. Choose the section you need, such as Emergency Help, Embassies, Rights, or News.
4. Follow the official source links whenever you need to confirm a detail.

You do not need an account.

## Using it without an internet connection

Some information you have already opened may remain available when you lose your connection. New news, current currency rates, outside websites, and some images still need internet access.

If an older saved currency rate is shown, the app tells you. Reconnect when you need the latest information.

## Your privacy

Preferences and saved information, including your travel map and optional map photo, are kept in your browser. Clearing your browser’s saved information may remove them. They do not automatically follow you to another device.

News, images, exchange rates, and official links use outside services. Opening those links takes you to the relevant provider’s website.

## Help keep the information useful

Contact details and services can change. Protect Probashi shows available sources and marks information that has not been verified. It cannot guarantee that every listing is complete or current.

If you spot a wrong number, an outdated address, a broken link, or another mistake, email **[hello@farhanbin.dev](mailto:hello@farhanbin.dev)**. Include the country, the office or page name, and an official source for the correction if you have one. You can also send suggestions to make the app easier to use.
